Getting started

Use the Administrator Portal to manage the AIH functions permitted by your role. Institution Admins remain restricted to their own institution; Platform Admins have broader platform responsibilities; Superadmin authority remains separately protected.

Users and account access

  • Create or maintain accounts only within your administrative authority.
  • Assign the minimum role required for the user's responsibilities.
  • Use the approved reset or SSO workflow for login problems.
  • Never request or store a user's password, token, or API key.

Groups and academic structure

Departments, courses, groups, instructors, and learners provide the structure used for academic organization and authorization. Group membership must remain within the appropriate institution boundary.

RAG and documents

RAG Access Points identify controlled knowledge collections. RAG access policies and group relationships determine which users may retrieve from them. Stored documents are not automatically visible to every user.

Do not weaken authorization or security scanning to make a document accessible.

Academic Agents

Academic Agents are specialized educational or research experiences. Agent availability, tools, institutional knowledge, and model routing remain governed by platform policy and user authorization.

Institution administration

Institution Admins manage permitted users, instructors, groups, academic structure, and institution-scoped resources within their institution. They must not manage another institution's protected data.

Platform administration

Platform-level administrators may manage institutions and platform-wide configuration according to their capabilities. Permanent deletion and immutable Superadmin controls remain separately protected.

Common errors

  • Authentication required: sign in again.
  • Permission denied: verify role, institution, and scope.
  • Document unavailable: verify RAG scope and processing status.
  • Agent unavailable: verify role/institution entitlement.
  • Upload rejected: verify supported format and remove unsafe active content.

Troubleshooting and escalation

  1. Record the visible error and the operation being attempted.
  2. Verify the user's role, institution, group, and relevant capability.
  3. Verify the expected RAG or Academic Agent scope where applicable.
  4. Escalate unresolved platform defects without sending passwords, credentials, tokens, or sensitive infrastructure details.

Security and privacy boundaries

AIH Support is guidance, not an authorization bypass. Help cannot elevate roles, expose secrets, inspect unauthorized documents, reveal raw internal diagnostics, or modify security policy.
