AI Scholar Hub institutional administration

Administration overview

This portal manages AIH institutions, users, academic groups, RAG access, academic agents, and AI experience policies. Available functions depend on your administrative role.

Institutions and users

  • Superadmins manage the complete AIH platform.
  • Platform Admins manage institutions and their administrators.
  • Institution Admins manage users and instructors only within their institution.
  • Institution data must remain within its assigned institution context.

Groups and academic structure

Groups contain users and instructors from one institution. Administrative accounts are managed separately and are not ordinary group members. Departments, courses, and subgroups can be used to organize academic access.

RAG access

RAG Access Points identify institution-owned document collections. RAG Access Groups determine which groups, courses, departments, or selected users may retrieve information from those collections.

Academic agents and AI policy

Academic Agents define guided educational experiences. AI Experience Policy controls the model classes and routes available to institutions, roles, and agents.
