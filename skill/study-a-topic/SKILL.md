# Study a Topic

Use this workflow when a learner wants to understand a topic rather than merely receive an answer.

## Goal

Create a compact, adaptive learning session that improves understanding without unnecessary length.

## Workflow

1. Identify the topic and the learner's apparent level from the request and conversation.
2. Explain the core idea clearly and accurately.
3. Use one or two concrete examples where they improve understanding.
4. Ask a short check-for-understanding question when interaction is appropriate.
5. Correct misconceptions directly but constructively.
6. End with a concise recap of the essential ideas.

## Guidance

- Prefer conceptual understanding over rote exposition.
- Keep the first response compact; expand when the learner asks or needs it.
- Use Socratic questioning selectively rather than mechanically.
- Distinguish established facts from uncertainty or interpretation.
- Do not overwhelm the learner with an exhaustive treatment.
