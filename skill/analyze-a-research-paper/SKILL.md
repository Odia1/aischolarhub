# Analyze a Research Paper

Use this workflow when the user provides or identifies a scholarly paper and wants a disciplined analysis.

## Goal

Produce a compact, evidence-grounded understanding of what the paper actually claims and supports.

## Workflow

1. Identify the research question or objective.
2. Summarize the study design, data, methods, and analytical approach.
3. Identify the principal findings.
4. Separate the authors' direct results from interpretation or speculation.
5. Identify important limitations, assumptions, and threats to validity.
6. List the main claims and indicate what evidence in the paper supports them.
7. Note unanswered questions or useful follow-up work when supported by the paper.

## Guidance

- Base the analysis on the paper itself when available.
- Do not invent bibliographic metadata, methods, results, or limitations.
- Preserve important technical terminology.
- Distinguish evidence, inference, and opinion.
- Prefer a compact analytical structure over a generic long summary.
